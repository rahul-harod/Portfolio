About Me

I am a Ph.D. graduate from the Department of Civil Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Eswar Rajasekaran. My doctoral research focused on developing advanced machine learning–driven methodologies for generating all-weather, high spatiotemporal resolution Land Surface Temperature (LST) datasets over India.

I qualified GATE in Civil Engineering (2019, Score: 615) and secured All India Rank 2 in GATE 2026 (Geomatics Engineering). I am a recipient of the prestigious Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF), awarded for the quality and national relevance of my research.

My research interests lie at the intersection of thermal remote sensing, machine learning, and environmental applications — with a focus on LST retrieval, gap-filling, spatial downscaling, evapotranspiration estimation, urban heat island analysis, and crop stress monitoring.

Currently, I am working as a Research Scientist at Sagri Co. Ltd., Bangalore, contributing to carbon credit estimation and GHG accounting frameworks for sustainable agriculture.
