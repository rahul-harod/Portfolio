About

I am a Ph.D. graduate from the Department of Civil Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Eswar Rajasekaran. My doctoral research developed machine learning–driven frameworks for generating all-weather, high spatiotemporal resolution Land Surface Temperature (LST) datasets over India — addressing the dual constraints of cloud contamination and the spatial–temporal resolution trade-off inherent to satellite thermal observations.

My research sits at the intersection of thermal remote sensing, passive microwave data fusion, deep learning, and surface energy balance modelling. I am particularly interested in translating these methodological advances into operational tools for water resource management, agricultural monitoring, and urban climate analysis. I am a recipient of the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF), awarded in recognition of the quality and national relevance of my research.

Research Highlights

All-Weather LST

Random Forest framework integrating AMSR2 passive microwave brightness temperatures with thermal infrared, increasing valid LST observations by ~67% at 1 km daily resolution across India.

Spatial Downscaling

Deep learning models (ResNet18/50, XGBoost) downscaling MODIS LST from 1 km to 100 m, achieving R² of 0.95–0.97 and RMSE of 1.07–1.97 K across overpass times.

Evapotranspiration

Integration of all-weather LST into the SPARSE energy balance model improved monthly ET accuracy by ~27%, validated at 7 eddy covariance sites across India.

LST Retrieval

Evaluated VI-based emissivity models and retrieval algorithms for Landsat data; SMWA achieved RMSE of 1.70 K, outperforming operational Landsat Level-2 products (RMSE: 3.52 K).

Open to Collaborate

I welcome research collaborations with academic groups and institutions working on the following themes:

LST Algorithm Development

Advancing retrieval accuracy, emissivity estimation, and cloud reconstruction for thermal infrared and passive microwave sensors over diverse land cover types.

Evapotranspiration & Water Resources

Improving surface energy balance–based ET estimation using all-weather LST inputs, with applications to irrigation scheduling and basin-scale water resource modelling.

Crop Health & Agricultural Stress

Satellite-based monitoring of thermal anomalies for early detection of crop stress, drought impact assessment, and precision agriculture interventions.

Urban Heat Island Analysis

High-resolution LST time series analysis for UHI characterisation, seasonal dynamics, and surface–atmosphere interactions in Indian cities.
