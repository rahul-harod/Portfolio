About

I am a Ph.D. graduate from the Department of Civil Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Eswar Rajasekaran. My doctoral research focused on developing machine learning–based frameworks for generating all-weather, high spatiotemporal resolution Land Surface Temperature (LST) datasets over India, addressing the challenges of cloud contamination and the spatial–temporal resolution trade-off in satellite thermal observations.

My research lies at the intersection of thermal remote sensing, passive microwave data fusion, deep learning, and ET estimation. I am particularly interested in translating these advances into operational tools for water resource management, agricultural monitoring, and urban climate analysis. I hold the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF), awarded by the Ministry of Education on the basis of my research proposal and its national relevance, to support high-quality doctoral research.

Research Highlights

All-Weather LST

Random Forest–based framework integrating AMSR2 passive microwave brightness temperatures and thermal infrared data, increasing valid LST observations by ~67% at 1 km daily resolution across India.

Spatial Downscaling

Deep learning models (ResNet18/50, XGBoost) used to downscale MODIS LST from 1 km to 100 m, achieving R² of 0.95–0.97 and RMSE of 1.07–1.97 K across overpass times.

Evapotranspiration

Integration of all-weather LST into the SPARSE surface energy balance model improved weekly and monthly ET accuracy by ~13%, validated at 7 eddy covariance sites across India.

LST Retrieval

Systematic evaluation of VI-based emissivity models and retrieval algorithms for Landsat data; the SMWA achieved RMSE of 1.70 K, outperforming the operational Landsat Level-2 product (RMSE: 3.52 K).

Open to Collaborate

I welcome research collaborations with academic groups and institutions working on the following themes:

LST Algorithm Development

I am particularly interested in collaborative research on advanced LST retrieval and downscaling approaches, including ensemble modelling, multi-sensor data fusion, and improved cloud-gap reconstruction techniques for thermal infrared observations.

Evapotranspiration & Water Resources

Improving evapotranspiration estimation using high-resolution, all-weather LST products, with applications in irrigation scheduling, water resource assessment, and agricultural hydrology.

Crop Health & Agricultural Stress

Satellite-based thermal anomaly analysis for early detection of crop stress and monitoring of vegetation response under water-limited and drought conditions.

Urban Heat Island & Heatwave Analysis

High-resolution LST time series for urban heat island characterisation, spatiotemporal analysis of heatwave intensity and persistence, and assessment of surface–atmosphere interactions in urban environments.
