Teaching

Experience • Courses

My teaching experience spans laboratory instructions and outreach courses — primarily in remote sensing and geospatial data analysis. During my Ph.D. at IIT Bombay, I served as a Teaching Assistant across multiple courses and accumulated approximately 150 hours of PMRF-mandated teaching.

Teaching Assistant — IIT Bombay

2020 – 2025

Remote Sensing Laboratory

Conducted lab sessions for M.Tech. and Ph.D. students using Google Earth Engine (GEE) and QGIS — covering satellite image operations (filter, clip, mosaic), land cover classification, LST and emissivity retrieval, vegetation index computation, time-series export, zonal statistics, and data visualization.

Advanced Surveying & Photogrammetry Laboratory

Instructed B.Tech. students in field surveying techniques — compass traversing, digital levelling, total station, and theodolite operations — and photogrammetry concepts including relief displacement, stereo image interpretation, 3D viewing, and parallax measurement.

Examination Invigilation

Served as invigilator for B.Tech. and M.Tech. semester examinations.

PMRF Teaching Outreach
