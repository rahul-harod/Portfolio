Teaching Philosophy

I believe in creating an innovative, application-driven learning environment where students connect theoretical concepts with real-world problems. My approach integrates:

  • Problem-based learning using real satellite datasets and environmental challenges
  • Hands-on laboratory sessions with open-source tools (QGIS, Python, Google Earth Engine)
  • Numerical problem-solving to build conceptual depth
  • Machine learning and AI integration in coursework
  • Collaborative mini-projects and student presentations
  • Weekly assignments using live geospatial datasets

I aim to prepare students with high-demand geospatial and data analysis skills essential for modern civil engineering practice in water resource, environmental, and climate domains.
